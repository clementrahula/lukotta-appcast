Version 1.22.21-beta.1

  • Every file and folder on an open drive can be opened, changed and deleted in Finder, whatever permissions the drive stored.
  • An open drive appears in the Finder sidebar under Favourites, and leaves it when the drive is ejected.
  • A USB drive macOS reads as APFS is no longer listed as a drive to unlock.
