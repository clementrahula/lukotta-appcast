Version 1.15.1

  • Opens BitLocker, LUKS and NTFS drives that macOS cannot read on its own, and hands them back to Finder read and write.
  • Opens virtual machine disks: qcow2, VMDK, VDI, VHD and VHDX, and any BitLocker or LUKS volume inside one.
  • Writes qcow2, VMDK, VDI and VHD images as well as reading them, so files can be copied into a virtual machine's disk. Writing them is untested and the app says so before opening one. A VHDX is read and never written.
  • Every drive and image can be opened read-only, from the button beside Open. A drive that cannot be written to opens read-only rather than not at all, and says why.
  • Can put back what was open after a restart, from the setting at the top of Settings. Off until you turn it on.
  • Twenty-one languages besides English.
