Version 1.22.0

  • Copying a lot of files no longer stalls, and the folder you are copying into keeps responding.
  • Nothing is left out of a copy without saying so.
  • A drive Windows left in a bad state is repaired, so you can write to it again.
  • A drive you saved a password for opens without asking again.
  • Saving the password is offered by default.
  • Linux drives open, including XFS and drives that are part of a RAID array.
  • Every disk image the app can open can be written to and read back.
  • A drive opens the first time you use the app.
  • The early development notice appears once.
