Version 1.22.20

  • Opening a drive shows what is happening and about how long is left.
  • The first open after an update no longer waits while the Linux environment is set up.
  • A saved password is found by every Lukotta app and is never asked for again.
  • Every Lukotta app remembers every drive it has opened.
  • Finder shows each drive under its own name, and deleting a folder deletes every file in it.
  • A drive is no longer closed by another installed copy of Lukotta.
  • Quitting shows which drive is being ejected, and no longer hangs.
  • A drive ejected in Finder is closed in Lukotta too.
